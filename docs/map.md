# Course map

Nine levels from Day 0 to your capstone. Every week is a level; its Friday checkpoint is the **boss**: beat it with 70 % or more to clear the level and win that week's voucher. A day turns green when you pass its quiz, a star means you solved its daily challenge, and somewhere in every week hides a **lucky day** — you will know it when you open it.

Nothing is really locked: you can open any day from here or from the menu. The lock only shows how far ahead it is.
